    Before you start — read this

    No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Once a story mission is done you cannot select it again. Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission (15 manual slots + 1 autosave).

    One missable: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save until it pops. Everything else stays available after the credits.

    100% checklist only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — but work you do earlier still counts.
